Hold your LEFT hand at the MAXIMUM push depth (closest to camera) and use your other hand to click CONFIRM.

HOW TO PLAY

HOVER PUSH (TILT)

Hold your hands over the board. To tilt the board, physically push your hand towards the webcam lens. The closer your hand gets to the camera, the harder it pushes down on that side of the maze! (Use Calibration to set your max depth).

ROTATE CAMERA

Pinch your fingers with ONE hand and drag. The camera will smoothly orbit around the board, allowing you to view the maze from any angle.

ZOOM IN / OUT

Pinch your fingers with BOTH hands simultaneously. Pull your hands apart to zoom into the specific area between your hands, or push them together to zoom out.

GOAL

Navigate the white ball to the Green Beacon at the end of the maze. Avoid landing on the glowing red hazard tiles, or you will respawn at the start!
